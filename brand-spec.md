# TrustBridge Escrow — Brand Spec

## Visual Direction: Editorial Monocle
*Luxury / Refined / Established*

### 1. Color Palette (OKLch)
- `--bg`:      `oklch(98% 0.004 95)` (Paper)
- `--surface`: `oklch(100% 0.002 95)` (Bright Paper)
- `--fg`:      `oklch(20% 0.018 70)` (Ink)
- `--muted`:   `oklch(48% 0.012 70)` (Dim Ink)
- `--border`:  `oklch(90% 0.006 95)` (Subtle Fold)
- `--accent`:  `oklch(52% 0.10 28)` (Oxblood Accent)

### 2. Typography
- **Display**: `'Iowan Old Style', 'Charter', Georgia, serif`
- **Body**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Mono**: `ui-monospace, 'IBM Plex Mono', monospace` (Metadata and kicker text only)

### 3. Layout Posture
- **Serif Display**: Headlines use generous whitespace and large serif weights.
- **Hairline Borders**: 1px solid `--border` or `--fg` for structure. No shadows.
- **Zero Radius**: Sharp corners (0-2px) to convey architectural precision and legal trust.
- **Kickers**: Uppercase mono text for labels and eyebrows.
- **Accent Budget**: Used at most twice per screen for primary CTAs or status highlights.

### 4. Compliance & Trust
- Prominent display of GDPR and PCI-DSS badges.
- Lock icons for encrypted sections.
- Tabular figures for financial data.
