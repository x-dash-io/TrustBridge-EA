Our Process

How TrustBridge Secures Your Transaction

We eliminate the counterparty risk in online exchanges by acting as a neutral third party that holds funds until all conditions are met.

Terms are Agreed

Buyer and Seller agree to terms including inspection period and milestones.

Buyer Funds Escrow

Funds are held in a secure, audited account. Seller is notified to proceed.

Service or Goods Delivered

Seller provides the agreed-upon value. Buyer initiates the inspection period.

Funds Released

Upon verification or completion of terms, funds are released to the seller.
